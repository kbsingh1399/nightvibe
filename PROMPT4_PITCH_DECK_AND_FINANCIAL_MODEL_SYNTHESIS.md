## 4. The 5 Top Failure Modes & How We Defend Them

1. **Owner Distrust of Formalization / Cash Arbitrage:**
   - *Risk:* 30–40% of clubs resist digital records due to off-books cash traditions.
   - *Defense:* Position as a **SaaS yield tool for unsold late-night tables and verified guestlists**, not a replacement for their entire private VIP table business.
2. **Weekend Concentration Risk (70–80% GMV on Fri/Sat):**
   - *Risk:* High fixed team costs with revenue clustered across only 2–3 nights.
   - *Defense:* Diversify into **weekday college exam takeovers, techno sundowners, corporate team bookings, and F&B brunches**.
3. **Promoter Multi-Homing & Showrooming:**
   - *Risk:* PR uses app to meet guests, then takes payment in cash at the door.
   - *Defense:* **System-Only Doors:** Bouncers turn away cash-at-door walk-ins unless registered via verified dynamic QR.
4. **Regulatory / Curfew Fragility (Mumbai/Bangalore 1:00 AM Curfews):**
   - *Risk:* Sudden police raids or licensing changes shutdown revenue.
   - *Defense:* Dual-city operation (**Mumbai + North Goa**) to balance seasonal/regulatory swings.
5. **Consumer Convenience Fee Resistance:**
   - *Risk:* Guests accustomed to free WhatsApp lists balk at app fees.
   - *Defense:* Ensure dynamic bidding gives the guest **perks worth ₹300–₹500 (free shots, queue skip)** that far exceed the ₹50–₹90 convenience fee.
